\section{Basic Text Emphasis}

This is \textbf{bold text} and this is \textit{italic text}. We can also use \emph{emphasized text} which adapts to context.

Alternative commands: \bf{bold}, \it{italic}, \em{emphasis}, \rm{roman}, \sf{sans serif}, \tt{typewriter}.

\textsc{Small Caps Text} and \textsl{slanted text} provide additional emphasis options.

\section{Font Families and Styles}

\textrm{Roman family text} is the default.
\textsf{Sans serif family text} for modern look.
\texttt{Typewriter family text} for code.

\textup{Upright shape}, \textit{Italic shape}, \textsl{Slanted shape}, \textsc{Small caps shape}.

\textmd{Medium weight}, \textbf{Bold weight}.

\section{Hyphenation and Line Breaking}

Long words can be hyphenated automatically: supercalifragilisticexpialidocious.

Manual hyphenation: su\-per\-cal\-i\-frag\-i\-lis\-tic\-ex\-pi\-al\-i\-do\-cious.

Prevent hyphenation: \mbox{supercalifragilisticexpialidocious}.

\section{Special Environments}

\begin{quote}
This is a quote environment for shorter quotations.
It provides appropriate indentation and spacing.
\end{quote}

\begin{quotation}
This is a quotation environment for longer quotations.
It's similar to quote but with paragraph indentation.

Multiple paragraphs can be included in quotations
with proper formatting maintained.
\end{quotation}

\begin{verse}
This is a verse environment\\
For poetry and similar content\\
Where line breaks are important\\
And spacing is preserved.
\end{verse}

formatting and spacing. The abstract is usually placed after
